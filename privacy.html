Privacy Policy

Bots by Rubenvst21 · Last updated 4 October 2026

"Bots" is a personal automation tool run by its owner. It makes short videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What it uses

  • YouTube API Services, with the single permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) to the owner's channel.
  • The sign-in token the owner grants, stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and used only to upload the owner's videos.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone.
  • It does not read viewers' data, comments, subscribers or watch history.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

Google and YouTube

Uploads go through YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply. The owner can remove the tool's access at any time on the Google security settings page.
